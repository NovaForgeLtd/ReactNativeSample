iOSNova brand asset mapping:
- assets/icon.png: glossy dark iOSNova launcher/store icon
- assets/splash-icon.png: blue iOSNova monogram for splash/branding
- assets/android-icon-foreground.png: blue monogram adaptive foreground
- assets/android-icon-background.png: dark adaptive background
- assets/android-icon-monochrome.png: monogram monochrome mask
- assets/favicon.png: glossy iOSNova favicon

The app catalogue hard-maps iOSNova bundle identifiers to the bundled launcher icon so stale repository thumbnails cannot replace the iOSNova brand artwork.
